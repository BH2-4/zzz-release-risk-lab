# Ingested Constraints

## Evidence
- source: `docs/mvp-v1-spec.md`
- constraint: Every research conclusion binds to a public source; C/F claims stay out of the default AI pack.
- constraint: Synthetic assumptions live outside the evidence ledger and remain explicit in compiled artifacts.
- constraint: Non-neutral regional coefficients require direct evidence.

## Representation
- source: `docs/mvp-v1-spec.md`
- constraint: Synthetic agents are not real player samples and are not demographically representative.
- constraint: Scenario scores and sensitivity intervals are not reality probabilities.

## Model
- source: `docs/mvp-v1-spec.md`
- constraint: BYOK credentials enter only a trusted Node process and never the repository or browser.
- constraint: Only a completed live request with provider, model, request ID, and validated output may set `realModelUsed: true`.
- constraint: Do not use an LLM to emit the final risk probability or perform 125 x 42 model calls.

## Chain
- source: `docs/mvp-v1-spec.md`
- constraint: Do not store corpora on chain or create gambling, speculation, or economically valuable prediction markets.
- constraint: Deployment and wallet transactions remain user-confirmed testnet operations.
